### Top 8 IT risk metrics

- Number of risks identified
- Volume of risks occurred
- Cost of risk management
- Percentage of risks mitigated
- Percentage of risks monitored
- Risk tolerance vs exposure
- Internal vs external vulnerabilities
- Uptime
