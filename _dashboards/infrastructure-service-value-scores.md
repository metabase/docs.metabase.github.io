summary: Infrastructure service value scores are metrics that give you insight into the end-user’s perceived value of your products and services. Keeping a good balance of high-quality products and low-effort solutions to problems that might arise is key to having successful customer retention. If your scores are low, that indicates a pain area that your team will need to address. It’s a simple process that gives you valuable insight into the minds of your customers.
calculate: "Figuring out your infrastructure service value scores is as simple as creating a small survey. You can ask customers after a customer service interaction how your product makes them feel in a couple of statements like this: “After talking to customer service, I feel like I can use this product better than before.” or “After talking to customer service, I’m confident about my decision to choose this product.” You can have a rating system where you ask customers to agree or disagree with those statements on a scale. Then, you’ll have a general idea of how much perceived value your product has to your customers."
