### Top 8 SOC monitoring metrics

- Operational health of infrastructure components
- Number of cybersecurity tickets and incidents
- Severity of cybersecurity tickets
- Time to cybersecurity threat detection
- Time to cybersecurity threat response
- Runs by framework
- Success and failure rate of queries created in last 30 days
- Mean time to close a case
