Make sure people only see what they need to

Granular permissions to keep people safe from accidentally seeing (or breaking) something they shouldn’t, or blasting raw user PII into a company all-hands deck.

  • Limit access at the table, row or column level
    Lock down sensitive tables, fields, and columns while lettings folks use the rest of the data.

  • Manage role-based permissions with SSO
    Set role-based visibility and permissions (e.g. marketing sees X, support sees Y) with separate view and edit access.
  • Keep track of who’s doing what, when
    You can even set alerts to monitor access and downloads on your usage analytics.
