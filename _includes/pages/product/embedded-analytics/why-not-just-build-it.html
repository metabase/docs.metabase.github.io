Why not just build it?

DIY and Charting libraries:
  • Seems simple at first but quickly balloons in scope. Think permissions, new visualization types, arbitrary filters, sorting, export buttons (psst, are you still awake?)
  • Requires ongoing dev time for maintenance, bug fixes, security, and scaling.
  • No built-in permissions, multi-tenancy, self-service analytics, or exports.
Other Embedded Analytics solutions:
  • Steeper learning curve for users to be able to run reports on their own.
  • Can still take months to get to POC.
Metabase:
  • Fastest time to value — get started in hours
  • Cost-effective — priced transparently, with no vendor lock-in
  • Built-in security — granular permissions, and multi-tenancy
  • Open-source flexibility with deployment options
