Not sure which embedding option is for you?

Interactive Embedding
Available in Pro and Enterprise plans.
  • Full, self-service report builder in in your app, with your branding.
  • Set SSO permissions so customers can query, visualize, and drill-down on the data they need, and nothing they don‘t.
Static Embedding
Available in all Metabase plans.
  • Simple dashboard or chart embedded in your app.
  • Your users will see data, but won’t be able to click through into it.
Embedded analytics SDK for React
Available in Pro and Enterprise plans.
  • Theme and embed individual React components for analytics that’s fully integrated with your UI.
  • Manage permissions and interactivity at both the component- and user-level.
