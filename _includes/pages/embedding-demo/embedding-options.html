Not sure which embedding option is for you?

Modular Embedding
Available in Pro and Enterprise plans.
  • JS library with a no-code UI to customize and embed interactive Metabase components for authenticated users.
  • Let your customers filter, click into, and explore data on their own.
  • Guest embeds for unauthenticated users can also be implemented with Modular Embedding on all plans.
Modular Embedding SDK for React
Available in Pro and Enterprise plans.
  • Theme and embed individual React components for analytics that’s fully integrated with your UI.
  • Manage permissions and interactivity at both the component- and user-level.
Full-app Embedding
Available in Pro and Enterprise plans.
  • Embed all of Metabase in your app with your branding.
  • Set SSO permissions so customers can query, visualize, and drill-down on the data they need, without giving access to anything they don‘t.
