How to analyze CSVs in Metabase

1
Start a free trial of Metabase with a built-in database. You don’t need to have a database already set up, a technical team, or anyone to help you. (But you can also use CSV upload with some databases in Metabase).
2
Have your CSV data available to upload and ready to analyze. If you have a Google or Excel spreadsheet, go to File > Download (.csv).
3
In Metabase, navigate to the Collection you want to add your CSV to.
4
Upload your CSV into the Collection by clicking {% include svg-icons/upload.html %} or dragging the file directly into the Collection you want to add it to.
5
Once your upload finishes, Metabase will create a model for that CSV data, as well as a table in the schema your admin has designated to store uploads. Now you’re ready to start querying, applying x-rays, building dashboards, and more.
6
Keep the model up to date as the data evolves. Upload new rows to the CSV-created model by clicking {{upload-icon}} in the collection view, and selecting ‘Append data to a model’.
