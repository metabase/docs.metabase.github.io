Summary

In this webinar hosted by Product Marketing Manager, Jess, Metabase Product Managers, Conor Dewey and Luiz Arakaki presented new features in Metabase 49.

Conor showcased new visualization improvements, including Sunburst pie charts, dashboard enhancements, and external content integration. He also covered the new download UI for exporting data.

Luiz presented on the new Databricks driver, schema user provisioning, and the cleanup feature for collections.

Alex talked about time granularity on dashboards, the ability to create metrics, and new exploration options for SQL questions.

Alberto focused on embedding improvements, introducing a new SDK for embedding in React applications, and enhancements to downloads and PDF exports.

Overall, Metabase 51 is packed with updates to make your data exploration smoother, your dashboards more powerful, and your workflows even more efficient. Read more about it in our release announcement.
