Built to play well with your data stack

Metabase fits with tools you’re already using to take your data from black box to accessible insights, while staying in control over who sees what.

  • Plug into your data source with official, partner and community connectors to over 20 databases and warehouses.
  • Manage access and map user groups with Single Sign On via SAML, JWT, and advanced LDAP.
  • Keep confidential data on a need-to-know basis with row- and column- level permissions via data sandboxes.
