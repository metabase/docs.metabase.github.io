Compliance, auditability, and trust-by-default

Metabase Enterprise supports strict compliance requirements by keeping data fully inside your controlled environment and aligning with industry-standard security practices.

  • SOC1 and SOC2 Type II foundation for cloud-managed deployments
  • Supports GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA-aligned deployments when self-hosted
  • Full audit logging for queries, dashboard access, and permission changes
  • SSO/SAML/SCIM for centralized identity and access lifecycle management
  • Usage analytics to track adoption and governance
  • No data ingestion or replication minimizing compliance scope
