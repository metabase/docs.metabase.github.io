Enterprise-ready security and data segregation

Designed for environments where data boundaries are non-negotiable, whether you need to isolate data for customers or teams.

  • Your data stays on your servers. Metabase doesn’t ingest your data, extract, store it, or do refreshes. Your queries are run against data stored in your database, not pulled in Metabase.
  • Fine-grained access control. Set strict visibility rules across databases, tables, rows, and columns to isolate customer and team data.
  • Enterprise identity management. Use SSO and automated user provisioning to control access without maintaining separate accounts.
