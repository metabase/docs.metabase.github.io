## What is Metabase's data reference section?

![The data reference section showing details on the Orders table in the Sample Database](/glossary/images/data-reference/data-reference.png)

Metabase's data reference section, found at `/reference/databases` or by clicking on the book icon throughout Metabase, lets you add and look up metadata about your databases and their tables and fields.
