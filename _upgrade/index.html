Frequently asked questions

What should I expect in the upgrade process?
Upgrading is pretty straight-forward. If you’re coming from open-source Metabase, just sign up for a free trial of any plan, and follow the migration guide. Everything will be included in the migration. If you’re already using a premium plan, just reach out and we can assist.

How do I know what’s included in each plan?
Our pricing page has all the details of every feature and benefit in each plan.

Can you tell me more about your cloud hosting?
We’ve got a great blog post covering everything you might want to know about Metabase Cloud.

Can I cancel my subscription?
Yup, and all of our plans start with a 14-day free trial. So it’s easy to try it out and make sure everything’s working for you. If you cancel, you can continue using your open-source instance, no problem.
