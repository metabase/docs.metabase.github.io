Founded in 2019, Valiotti Analytics provides a comprehensive suite of services covering data warehouse design, reports, dashboards, and predictive modeling. The company’s clientele includes Worldcoin, Refocus, Wing, and Aircall, reflecting its global reach and diverse specialization with a focus on EdTech, FinTech, Crypto, Telecom, and SaaS startups.

Valiotti Analytics offers Metabase integration as part of its tailored analytics stacks for simplified business intelligence.
With tailored integration, your company gains the ability to effortlessly query and visualize data, translating complex information into actionable insights. Valiotti Analytics facilitates the creation of dynamic dashboards, allowing teams to interact with data in formats that resonate, such as bar charts and detailed tables. This not only simplifies comprehension but also empowers non-technical users to actively participate in data-driven decision-making.

Valiotti Analytics enhances collaboration through Metabase's embedded charting and sharing functionalities. Your team can seamlessly share questions, dashboards, and findings, fostering a cohesive approach to decision-making across departments. Customization is another hallmark of our integration process, where Metabase can be tailored to mirror your organization's unique data needs and visualization preferences.
