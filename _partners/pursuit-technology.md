## About Pursuit Technology

Based in beautiful Yorkshire, UK, Pursuit Technology have been providing independent data consultancy since 2007 for companies across the UK and Europe. They work with both open-source and proprietary software to perfectly meet your Business Intelligence requirements.

Pursuit Technology's team puts your data to work to improve your productivity. Whether your data is locked in a cloud application or in a complex on-premise database, they ensure you can access your data, analyze it, visualize it, and act on it.
