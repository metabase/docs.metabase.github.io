2. **Custom Metabase Dashboards.** Milhos creates intuitive, insightful dashboards that blend data from multiple sources, including Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Instagram, TikTok, and email marketing platforms, providing comprehensive marketing analytics across all major channels.
3. **Data Transformation & Cleaning.** Milhos' expertise in ETL processes, including dbt (data build tool) with Metabase, ensures your data from various sources is clean, consistent, and ready for analysis.
4. **Performance Monitoring.** Milhos sets up Metabase to track key e-commerce KPIs, combining data from various Metabase connectors to give you a comprehensive view of your online presence.
5. **Scalable Data Architecture.** Milhos designs and implement robust data architectures that grow with your business, ensuring Metabase can handle increasing data volumes and sources.
