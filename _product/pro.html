See who did what, when, with comprehensive usage analytics

Analyze your Metabase usage directly in Metabase. Meet compliance needs, and make better-informed decisions to enable self-service.

  • A collection of dashboards, questions, and models. See trends on usage and performance, with interactive tools to drill deeper.
  • Set up subscriptions and alerts on user behavior. Get regular updates, or trigger an alert if something unusual happens.
  • Housekeeping for your Metabase. Make your most-used dashboards, questions, and models easier to find. Cache or ditch slow and unused content.

Grant row-level SQL permissions with connection impersonation

  • Configure database-side roles (think USE ROLE or SET ROLE) when querying.
  • Give a group access to the native/SQL editor, while restricting the group's access to data based on a specific database role.
  • Centralize your teams’ permissions on the database (currently available with Postgres, Redshift, and Snowflake).
