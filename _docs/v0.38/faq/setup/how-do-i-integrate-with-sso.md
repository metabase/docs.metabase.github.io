# How do I integrate Metabase and our single-sign on (SSO) option?

The open-source edition of Metabase allows you to [integrate with LDAP or Google sign-in](../../administration-guide/10-single-sign-on). If you’re using a [SAML](../../enterprise-guide/authenticating-with-saml) or [JWT](../../enterprise-guide/authenticating-with-jwt) SSO solution, you will need the [Enterprise Edition](/enterprise/).
